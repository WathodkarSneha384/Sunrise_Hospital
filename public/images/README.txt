LOGO FILE
=========
Place your hospital logo here as:

  logo.png

Recommended:
  - PNG with transparent background (not black box on white navbar)
  - Width at least 400px
  - Teal/cyan colors from your brand

After replacing the file:
  1. Open src/lib/site.ts and increase LOGO_VERSION (e.g. "2" → "3")
  2. Restart dev server: stop npm run dev, then run again
  3. Hard refresh browser: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
